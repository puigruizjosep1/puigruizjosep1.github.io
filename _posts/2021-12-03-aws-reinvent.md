> **TL;DR:** AWS re:Invent 2021 featured one of the most significant announcements for financial markets infrastructure in recent years: Nasdaq and AWS unveiled a multi-year partnership to bring market infrastructure to the cloud. On the machine learning front, AWS introduced tighter Amazon EMR integration in SageMaker Studio, launched the free SageMaker Studio Lab, and announced a new scholarship program to help broaden access to AI and ML education.

---

## Nasdaq and AWS: Bringing Capital Markets to the Cloud

One of the biggest moments of the conference came when Nasdaq CEO Adena Friedman joined AWS CEO Adam Selipsky on stage to announce a multi-year strategic partnership between Nasdaq and AWS.

The collaboration marks a significant step toward modernizing capital markets infrastructure by leveraging cloud technologies. Beyond migrating market infrastructure, the partnership also explores opportunities across Nasdaq's anti-financial crime platforms, data and analytics offerings, and market technology solutions.

For an industry where reliability, resilience, and performance are paramount, this announcement represents an important milestone in the ongoing evolution of financial markets.

## SageMaker Studio Meets Amazon EMR

AWS also announced a long-awaited improvement for data scientists working with large-scale data processing.

Amazon SageMaker Studio now provides native integration with Amazon EMR, allowing users to create, discover, manage, and connect to EMR clusters directly from the Studio interface—even across shared AWS accounts within an organization.

The integration also includes built-in access to Spark UI, making it easier to monitor, troubleshoot, and debug distributed Spark workloads. Together, these improvements significantly streamline the experience of building machine learning pipelines that rely on large-scale data processing.

## SageMaker Studio Lab: Free Machine Learning for Everyone

In another announcement focused on accessibility, AWS introduced the public preview of Amazon SageMaker Studio Lab.

The free service allows anyone to learn and experiment with machine learning without needing an AWS account, a credit card, or prior cloud infrastructure experience. By lowering the barrier to entry, Studio Lab provides an accessible environment for students, hobbyists, and aspiring practitioners to gain hands-on experience with ML.

Since access is initially limited, interested users should consider requesting an account early.

## Building a More Inclusive AI Community

One announcement that resonated beyond the technology itself was AWS's new scholarship program aimed at helping underrepresented and underserved students prepare for careers in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

According to the Pew Research Center, Hispanic workers make up only 8% of the STEM workforce in the United States. Increasing representation in AI and technology requires expanding access to education and opportunities, and initiatives like this scholarship program are a meaningful step in that direction.

The program is expected to support more than 2,000 students each year, helping create a broader and more diverse pipeline of future AI and ML professionals.
